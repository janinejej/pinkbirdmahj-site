Support

Pink Bird Mahj is a small company, and mail here is read by a person who works on the app. Questions, bug reports and disagreements about rulings are all welcome.

Email us

info@pinkbirdmahj.com

We aim to reply within two business days.

Reporting a problem

If something looks wrong, the most useful things to include are:

  • Which screen or drill you were on.
  • What you expected to happen, and what happened instead.
  • A screenshot, if you can take one.
  • Your phone model and OS version.

If you think a hand was judged incorrectly

Please tell us — this is the report we most want to receive. Include the tiles you held and the hand you were going for, and we will check it against the card. Rule correctness is the thing the app has to get right before anything else matters.

Common questions

Do I need a National Mah Jongg League card?

Yes. An official NMJL card is required to play, and must be purchased from the League, an authorized store, or a dealer. The app works alongside your physical card and never displays or replaces it.

What happens when the new card comes out each April?

The app is updated for the new card, and then Same Bones and Color the Hand become your trainer for it. Both are badged New card? Start here for exactly this reason: Same Bones shows you the shapes the new hands are built from, and Color the Hand drills their suit rules. Between them you learn to read this year's card rather than memorise it.

That is also why the work you put in last year is not wasted. The drills train patterns rather than specific hands, so the reading skill carries straight over — it is the shapes that change, not the ability to see them.

Is the app affiliated with the National Mah Jongg League?

No. Pink Bird Mahj: Card Coach is an independent app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the NMJL.

Does the tile scanner keep my photos?

No. A photo is sent for tile recognition and then discarded. See the privacy policy for the detail.

When is it available?

Card Coach is in active development for iPhone and Android. Email us and we will let you know when it is ready to try.
