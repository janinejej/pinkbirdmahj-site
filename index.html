Drills, not lessons

Learn to read the card — not memorize it.

Pink Bird Mahj: Card Coach is a practice app for American Mahjong players who already know how to play and want to get better. It trains the decisions you actually face at the table, using the National Mah Jongg League card you play with.

Why another mahjong app?

Most American Mahjong apps are either a place to play or a course to sit through. Both leave the same gap: the player who can already play, has plateaued, and wants to win more often.

Card Coach is built for that player. Rather than a fixed curriculum, it is a set of drills — each one isolating a single in-game judgment and making you practice it against real board positions. And rather than teaching this year's hands by rote, it teaches the patterns underneath them, so the skill carries forward when a new card arrives each April.

The Practice Table

The drill suite. Each one trains a different decision.

Same Bones

Block vision — seeing that very different-looking hands share the same underlying shape.

Color the Hand

The suit rule. Which groups must share a suit, which must differ, and why.

Joker Moves

Call or pass? The joker economy, with the discipline traps that make it hard.

Pivot Hands

Knowing when your line is dead and which hand to switch to.

Two Roads

Hand selection — play a deal out, then replay the identical deal down the other road.

Complete the Hand

What are you waiting on? Three levels, ending in a live table read.

Name That Hand

Given thirteen tiles, name the directions worth committing to.

Scan & Slay

Point your camera at a real rack and check it against the card, mid-game.

Coach Starling

The drills are graded by a coach who watches how you actually play — which decisions you get right, which ones you keep missing — and points you at the practice that fixes your particular weak spot rather than a fixed curriculum's next chapter.

You bring the card

An official National Mah Jongg League card is required to play, and must be purchased from the League, an authorized store, or a dealer. Card Coach works alongside your physical card — it never displays or replaces it. The app points you back to the card, because reading it fluently is the skill being taught.

About Pink Bird Mahj LLC

Pink Bird Mahj LLC is an independent software company registered in Texas, United States. We build one thing: tools that make American Mahjong players better at the game.

The bird was painted by a child in the founder's family, years before any of this. It was not a mahjong picture. It was not meant to be anything in particular — just a round pink bird with a heart on its head, liked and kept for its own sake.

The meaning arrived later. Once mahjong took hold and this company started, it turned out the right emblem had been on the wall the whole time. It is not a specific species and it was never meant to be tidy, which is precisely why we chose it: American Mahjong is a serious game played around a table by people enjoying themselves, and a slightly lopsided bird felt closer to that than anything polished would have.
