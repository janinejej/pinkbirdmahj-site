Privacy Policy

Pink Bird Mahj: Card Coach · Last updated 3 September 2026

Pink Bird Mahj LLC ("we", "us") makes the mobile application Pink Bird Mahj: Card Coach (the "app"). This policy explains what the app does and does not collect. We have tried to write it in plain language rather than boilerplate.

The short version. The app has no accounts and no login. Your practice history stays on your phone. The only thing that ever leaves your device is a photo you deliberately take with the tile scanner, which is sent for tile recognition and not retained afterwards. We do not sell data, and we do not use advertising or third-party analytics.

Information stored on your device

The app keeps the following in local storage on your phone. It is not transmitted to us, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app:

  • Your drill progress, levels and streaks.
  • Your game and hand statistics, including which hands you have attempted.
  • Your player profile — the skill estimates used to recommend which drill to practise next.
  • Your settings and preferences.

The tile scanner and your camera

The app can use your camera so you can photograph a rack of tiles and have them identified. The camera is used only when you choose to open the scanner, and the app asks your permission first.

When you take a scan, the photo is sent over an encrypted connection to an AI vision service for the sole purpose of recognising which tiles are in the image. The service returns a list of tiles and the app discards the rest. We do not store your photos, we do not build a library of them, and they are not used to train any model on our behalf.

If you never open the scanner, no image ever leaves your phone.

What we do not do

  • We do not require an account, an email address or a password.
  • We do not use advertising networks, and the app contains no ads.
  • We do not use third-party analytics or tracking software development kits.
  • We do not sell, rent or share personal information with data brokers.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.

Payments

If and when the app offers a paid subscription, purchases are handled entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play. Those companies process your payment under their own privacy policies. We never see or store your card details.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

You can revoke camera permission at any time in your phone's settings; the rest of the app continues to work without it. Because your practice data lives only on your device, uninstalling the app removes it. If you have a question about your information, write to us — we would rather answer it than have you guess.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and change the date at the top. Material changes will also be noted in the app.
